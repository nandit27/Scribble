## Direction contract

THESIS: Scribble is a calm working field, not a dashboard. It refuses boxed-panel density in favor of one expansive paper surface with controls that recede until needed.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white drafting paper rests on a linen-gray desk. Cocoa ink, faded blue, soft coral, moss, and ochre mark active work. Floating cream controls use thin rules, soft shadows, generous rounding, and one deliberate charcoal voice state.

STORY: A student understands where their source is, where they can work, and how to ask the tutor without leaving the page. Tool state and saving status remain evident without competing with study material.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim brand bar spans the top. The document and whiteboard sit as large paper sheets below. The whiteboard owns the visual center; a compact floating tool rail sits inside its upper-left edge, tutor controls sit lower-right, and navigation/history float at opposite corners.

FORM: Light-table study folio, chosen to translate the user-pinned warm paper, expressive type, and Excalidraw-like affordances into an operate-first interface. Seed: f4a0e111.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
