# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students working through math problems with source PDFs, handwritten working, and short tutor questions.

## Product Purpose

Scribble keeps a student's source material, whiteboard work, and AI tutoring in one workspace. Success means a student can ask for help and immediately inspect the resulting hint, explanation, work check, or graph alongside their work.

## Positioning

Voice and typed tutor turns produce inspectable visual artifacts directly on the active study surface rather than only chat responses.

## Operating Context

Students open a study PDF or blank notes, draw or add notes on a whiteboard, ask short questions, and refer back to generated artifacts while solving problems.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current MVP supports local browser saving, PDF viewing, non-destructive highlight notes, pen/eraser/text tools, undo/redo, typed tutoring, optional Bolna voice calls, generated study artifacts, and bounded graphing. Preserve current tutoring flows and local-save behavior.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Scribble. The requested whiteboard surface should draw from Excalidraw's clarity, warm white and cream-white materials, and reference-led expressive typography.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing working workspace implementation in `components/workspace.tsx`. No external claims, testimonials, or product metrics are available.

## Product Principles

- Source material and active work stay visibly connected.
- Tutor output must be inspectable, not ephemeral.
- Drawing should feel immediate and low-friction.
- Student work remains local to the browser in this demo.
